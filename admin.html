📋 What's running Skycare — quick guide
Every system below keeps a different part of Skycare alive. Click Go there ↓ to jump to its full details and live numbers.
System What is it? (in plain English) What does it do for the app? Cost risk
🔥 Firebase
Blaze plan · Google		 Think of it as the app's brain and filing cabinet. It remembers everything — tasks, audits, who's working, shopping lists — and serves that info instantly when someone opens the app. Stores all app data. Without it, the app is a blank screen. ⚠ Watch it Go there ↓
📂 Storage
Firebase · Google		 Like a hard drive in the cloud. When someone takes a photo during an audit, or generates a PDF report, the file goes here. It just sits there until someone downloads it. Holds audit photos and generated PDF reports. ⚠ Check monthly Go there ↓
🌐 GitHub Pages
Microsoft · Free		 The address where your team opens Skycare. It's like a free web host — when you go to the app's URL, GitHub serves it to your phone instantly, from servers all over the world. Makes the app accessible at its URL, 24/7. ✅ Free forever Go there ↓
🐙 GitHub
Microsoft · Free		 A safe where every version of the app's code is stored forever. Every time your developer makes a change, a snapshot is saved here. If something breaks, you can go back to any older version. Stores the code. Every update to the app goes through here. ✅ Free forever Go there ↓
🔔 Notifications
FCM · Google · Free		 The "ding" service. When a shift starts, when coffee is ready, or when MX needs something — this is what makes every team member's phone buzz. Google runs this for free, forever, no limits. Sends push alerts to every team member's phone. ✅ Free forever Go there ↓
✦ Claude
Anthropic · Subscription		 The AI that writes all the code. Every button, every screen, every feature of Skycare was built by Claude. It's like having a developer available 24/7 who knows the entire codebase by heart. Builds and fixes every feature of the app. ✦ Monthly sub Go there ↓
⚡ Cloudflare
Worker · Free		 A tiny robot that wakes up every 45 minutes, checks how much of the database has been used, and writes the numbers you see on this dashboard. Without it, the usage bars above would stay empty. Keeps this admin page's numbers up to date. ✅ Free forever Go there ↓
